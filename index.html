Privacy Policy — PlaySuite

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetSage Studios (“NetSage Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with PlaySuite, our mobile game collection.

By using PlaySuite, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information PlaySuite May Process

Account and authentication information

PlaySuite can use Firebase Authentication to establish an app identity. An anonymous account may be created so supported online and cloud features can operate without requiring you to provide a name or email address.

PlaySuite also provides Google Sign-In functionality for supported account protection and recovery features. If you choose to use Google Sign-In, authentication information associated with your Google account may be processed by Google and Firebase Authentication. PlaySuite may receive information necessary to authenticate and associate your PlaySuite identity with that sign-in method.

We do not receive your Google account password.

Game, progression, and settings data

PlaySuite processes gameplay information such as game state, scores, statistics, achievements, progression, preferences, settings, and similar information necessary to provide game functionality.

Some information may remain locally on your device. Features that support cloud synchronization, multiplayer, account recovery, or other online functionality may transmit associated game or account data to our backend services.

Multiplayer and online feature data

When you use supported multiplayer or online features, PlaySuite may process identifiers, match or session information, game state, and other information necessary to create, join, synchronize, recover, or operate online sessions.

Purchase and entitlement information

PlaySuite offers an optional one-time purchase called PlaySuite+ through Google Play.

Google Play processes the financial transaction. NetSage Studios does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

PlaySuite and its backend services may process purchase-related information supplied by Google Play, such as product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens or derived identifiers, and entitlement status, for purposes including purchase verification, restoring access, preventing duplicate or fraudulent entitlement claims, and providing PlaySuite+ features.

Technical and security information

Our service providers may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and protect PlaySuite and its online services. Depending on the service and device, this may include app or device information, authentication identifiers, network information, integrity or security signals, and diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information processed through PlaySuite to:

  • provide and maintain PlaySuite's games and features;
  • save or synchronize supported game information;
  • provide authentication and account recovery;
  • operate supported multiplayer and online functionality;
  • verify PlaySuite+ purchases and maintain entitlements;
  • restore eligible purchases or account access;
  • secure PlaySuite and protect against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and invalid purchase claims;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve reliability; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell users' personal information.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

PlaySuite uses services provided by third parties to provide certain functionality.

Google Firebase — including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check, and related infrastructure used for authentication, online services, cloud functionality, security, and backend processing.

Google Sign-In — used when a user chooses to protect or recover supported PlaySuite account access through a Google account.

Google Play and Google Play Billing — used to distribute PlaySuite, process the optional PlaySuite+ purchase, report purchase state, and support purchase verification and restoration.

Information processed by these providers is also subject to their applicable privacy policies and terms.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce applicable terms, protect the security of PlaySuite or its users, or respond to valid legal process.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through PlaySuite.

Online communications use the security mechanisms provided by the relevant platforms and service providers. Access to cloud information is restricted through application and backend access controls appropriate to the applicable service.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Local game information may remain on your device until you reset applicable data, clear PlaySuite's application data, or uninstall the app.

Information associated with online services or an authenticated PlaySuite identity may be retained while necessary to provide PlaySuite functionality, maintain account or entitlement state, protect the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable requirements and our operational needs.

Certain records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, transaction integrity, legal compliance, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your PlaySuite account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion without using the PlaySuite app, email:

netsagestudios@gmail.com

Use the subject: PlaySuite Account Deletion Request

Please state that you are requesting deletion of your PlaySuite account. We may need to request information reasonably necessary to identify the applicable PlaySuite account and protect users against unauthorized deletion requests.

PlaySuite will also provide an in-app pathway for applicable users to initiate account deletion.

When an applicable PlaySuite account is deleted, data associated with that account will be deleted except for information that we are required or permitted to retain for legitimate purposes such as security, fraud prevention, transaction integrity, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Deleting a PlaySuite account does not necessarily delete transaction records maintained independently by Google Play under Google's own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

PlaySuite is a general-audience game collection and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

We do not knowingly use PlaySuite to solicit personal information from children. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances requiring parental consent and such consent was not obtained, we will take appropriate steps to delete that information.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information through PlaySuite may contact us using the information below.

8. Your Choices

Depending on the feature, you may be able to:

  • use supported portions of PlaySuite without linking a Google account;
  • choose whether to use Google Sign-In;
  • reset certain settings or local gameplay information;
  • request deletion of your PlaySuite account and associated data; and
  • contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Some online, synchronization, purchase-restoration, multiplayer, or account-recovery functionality may require processing necessary information in order to operate.

9. International Processing

Our service providers may process information on servers located in the United States or other countries. Where applicable, information may therefore be processed outside the country in which you reside, subject to the safeguards and legal requirements applicable to the relevant provider and processing activity.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PlaySuite's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes may also be communicated through PlaySuite or other appropriate means where required.
